\subsection{Trial File Format}

Each trial was saved as a JSON file with the fields listed below, alongside a line-delimited log of every raw message received from the streaming application.

\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\texttt{session\_id} & condition, participant and start time \\
\texttt{condition} & condition label, e.g.\ \texttt{baseline}, \texttt{isol\_l}, \texttt{spin\_r} \\
\texttt{participant\_id} & pseudonymous participant code \\
\texttt{recorded\_at} & ISO timestamp of trial start \\
\texttt{target\_duration\_s} & set trial duration in seconds \\
\texttt{sample\_count} & number of RR intervals received \\
\texttt{samples} & list of RR intervals with arrival timestamps \\
\texttt{computed\_stream} & real-time metrics reported by the streaming application \\
\end{tabular}

\subsection{Pre-session Questionnaire}
\label{sec:app-questionnaire}

The questionnaire takes about two minutes and is completed on a phone or laptop before the strap is fitted \confirm{final wording and form}.

\textbf{Demographics}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item Age (years)
\item Sex
\item Height (cm) and weight (kg)
\item Which hand do you write with? (left / right / both)
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Health and today}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em,resume]
\item Do you have a heart, lung or neurological condition? (yes / no)
\item Do you take medication that affects heart rate, such as beta-blockers? (yes / no)
\item Did you have coffee, tea or energy drinks in the last 2 hours? (yes / no)
\item Did you drink alcohol in the last 24 hours? (yes / no)
\item Did you do strenuous exercise in the last 24 hours? (yes / no)
\item How many hours did you sleep last night?
\item Do you smoke or vape? (no / occasionally / daily)
\item How many hours of physical exercise do you do in a typical week?
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Experience}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em,resume]
\item How long have you practised with the isolation stick? (never / under 6 months / 6--24 months / 2--5 years / over 5 years)
\item How long have you practised other flow-arts props (poi, staff, juggling, contact, hoop)? (same options)
\item In a typical week, how many hours do you practise any flow-arts prop?
\item How would you rate your isolations? (1 = cannot do them, 10 = expert)
\item How would you rate your spins? (1 = cannot do them, 10 = expert)
\item Can you keep isolations going for 2 minutes without dropping the stick? (yes / no / not sure)
\item Can you keep spins going for 2 minutes without dropping the stick? (yes / no / not sure)
\end{enumerate}
